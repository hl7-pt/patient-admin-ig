Official URL: http://example.com/fhir/hl7pt/StructureDefinition/address-pt Version: 1.0.0
Active as of 2026-03-03 Computable Name: PTAddress

Context of Use

    • Element ID: Address

Usage info

Usos:

  • Exemplos para este Extensão: Bundle/patient-link, Bundle/patient-new and Bundle/patient-update
